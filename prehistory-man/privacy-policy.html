Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

This policy describes how the mobile game Prehistoric Man (the “App”), developed by Viktor Panasiuk, handles your information.

Data stored on your device

Game progress, records, checkpoints and settings are stored only on your device. They are never sent to us or to third parties, and they are deleted when you uninstall the App or use Reset progress in Settings.

Crash reports

To find and fix bugs, the App sends crash and error reports to Sentry, a crash reporting service. A report may include:

  • the error and the state of the App when it happened (stack trace, screens visited, taps on UI elements);
  • device and system information (device model, operating system and version, App version, memory and storage state);
  • a random installation identifier.

Crash reports don’t include your name, email address, contacts, location or any other personal information, and IP addresses aren’t stored. The data is used only to diagnose and fix problems in the App and is deleted automatically under Sentry’s data retention period. See Sentry’s privacy policy.

What we don’t do

The App has no accounts, ads or analytics, doesn’t request access to your location, camera, microphone or contacts, and doesn’t sell or share your data.

Children

The App doesn’t knowingly collect personal information from children or anyone else.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
